# Attendly MVP Scope

## Goal

Build one complete working slice of Attendly that demonstrates how an AI receptionist can assist a real business.

## Business Configuration

The business owner should be able to configure:

- Business name
- Business description
- Industry
- Address
- Contact information
- Services/products
- Prices
- Opening hours
- FAQs
- Appointment rules
- AI receptionist personality

## AI Receptionist

The AI receptionist should:

- Understand customer messages
- Answer business questions
- Use configured business information
- Maintain conversation context
- Capture customer information
- Perform supported business actions
- Request human assistance when required

## Lead Management

Store:

- Customer name
- Phone number
- Source
- Conversation history
- Interested service/product
- Lead status
- Follow-up date
- Assigned employee

## Appointment Management

The system should support:

- Available working hours
- Availability checking
- Appointment creation
- Rescheduling
- Cancellation
- Confirmation messages

## Follow-ups

The system should be able to identify leads requiring follow-up and send configured messages at appropriate times.

## Dashboard

The dashboard should provide visibility into:

- Conversations
- Leads
- Appointments
- Follow-ups
- Customers
- AI activity
- Human handoffs
- Basic analytics

## Human Handoff

The AI should hand a conversation to a human when:

- It cannot confidently handle the request
- The customer requests a human
- The request requires human judgment

## MVP Boundary

The initial MVP should NOT attempt to build every possible business automation feature.

Voice calling, invoicing, advanced sales agents and additional integrations are planned for later stages.
