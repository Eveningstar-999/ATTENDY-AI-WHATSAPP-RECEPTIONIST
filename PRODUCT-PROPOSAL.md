# Attendly Product Proposal

## Problem

Small businesses spend significant time handling repetitive customer communication, answering common questions, collecting leads, scheduling appointments and following up with potential customers.

These tasks often require employees to manually respond to every customer.

## Solution

Attendly aims to provide businesses with AI employees that can communicate with customers and perform repetitive business tasks automatically.

The first product focuses on an AI WhatsApp receptionist.

## Target Customers

The initial target market is small and medium-sized service businesses, including:

- Clinics
- Salons
- Restaurants
- Car dealerships
- Real-estate agencies
- Pharmacies
- Other service businesses

## Core Value

Attendly helps businesses:

- Respond faster
- Capture more leads
- Reduce repetitive manual work
- Automate appointment scheduling
- Maintain customer conversations
- Follow up with potential customers

## Product Vision

The long-term vision is an AI business automation platform where businesses can create multiple specialized AI employees.

Examples:

- Receptionist
- Sales Agent
- Customer Support Agent
- Appointment Agent
- Follow-up Agent

The product will start with one focused use case and expand gradually.
